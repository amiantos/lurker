# Coming from The Lounge

Lurker has no importer for The Lounge yet. Networks and channels are added by
hand; see [Getting Started](/guide/getting-started). Importing The Lounge's
logs is something we're considering.

What you can carry over today is your nick colors.

## Nick colors

To give other people's nicks the colors they have in The Lounge, change three
settings under Settings › Appearance:

1. **Nick hash algorithm**: Sum (The Lounge).
2. **Trailing characters to ignore**: empty.
3. **Nick color palette**: one of the lists below.

Each Lounge theme has two lists. The exact one is The Lounge's own colors. The
tuned one keeps each color's hue and takes its lightness and saturation from
Lurker's theme.

Your own nick keeps its own color setting. Clearing the ignored characters also
means `bob_` and `bob` stop sharing an avatar tile. Applying a theme resets the
palette, so save the result as a theme of your own.
